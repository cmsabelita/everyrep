Questions

Which apps can EveryRep lock?

Any apps you choose in Apple's Screen Time picker, such as TikTok, Instagram or games. Phone, Messages, Maps and Camera always work.

What counts as a finished workout?

A logged workout of 10 minutes or more, or one with 4 working sets. When it counts, the apps open for the rest of the day.

What if I really need an app during training time?

You get three emergency unlocks a month, each opening the apps for 15 minutes. You can also turn the lock off in Settings.

Are my apps locked on rest days?

No. The lock follows your program's training days. If you move a workout to another day, the lock moves with it.

What happens if I skip a day?

By default the apps open at 10 pm anyway, so a skipped day never keeps them locked overnight.

Can EveryRep see which apps I use?

No. The lock uses Apple's Screen Time, so EveryRep never sees which apps you use or for how long.
