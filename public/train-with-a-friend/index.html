How it works

  1. Send your program

    During a workout, tap Train together, then Send QR.

  2. Your friend scans it

    In EveryRep, they open Train and tap Scan a friend's code, or open the link you sent.

  3. Same plan, two phones

    They preview the program, then use it now or save it for later.

Questions

How do I share my program?

During a workout, tap Train together, then Send QR. Your friend scans the code or opens the link.

How does my friend open it?

In EveryRep, open Train and tap Scan a friend's code, or open the shared link on their iPhone. They see the program first and choose whether to use it.

What gets shared?

Only the program: exercises, sets, reps, rest and the workout title. No name, no workout history, no Apple Health data.

Does my friend need an account?

No. EveryRep has no accounts. Your friend needs the app on an iPhone.
