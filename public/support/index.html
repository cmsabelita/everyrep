Common questions

My apps are still locked after my workout. Why?

A workout opens the apps when it counts: 10 minutes or more, or 4 working sets, and you tapped Finish workout. Short or empty workouts don't count.

How do I use an emergency unlock?

Tap the button on the EveryRep shield, or go to Settings. You get three a month, and each opens the apps for 15 minutes.

How do I turn the lock off?

Open Settings in EveryRep and turn off the training lock.

How do I turn online AI on or off?

Settings > Data & privacy > Online AI. With it off, workouts are built on your iPhone and nothing personal is sent.

How do I delete my data?

Settings > Data & privacy > Delete all data erases everything EveryRep stored on your iPhone.
