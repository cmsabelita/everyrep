How it works

  1. Get a program built for your week

    Answer a few questions in about a minute: your days, equipment and experience. EveryRep plans the workouts.

  2. Pick your training time and the apps to lock

    Choose the apps that pull you away with Apple's Screen Time picker.

  3. Training time comes, the apps wait

    On planned training days, the apps you chose show the EveryRep shield.

  4. Log the workout, the shield lifts

    Finish today's workout (10 minutes or 4 working sets) and the apps open for the rest of the day.

Why it sticks

A real program

Not random workouts: a plan matched to your days, equipment and experience. Move a workout and the lock moves with it.

A lock you control

Three emergency unlocks a month, and the lock can be turned off in Settings. Phone, Messages, Maps and Camera always work.

Streaks that count weeks

Your streak counts weeks in a row, so one missed day doesn't wipe out your progress.

Train with a friend

Share your program with a QR code, and your friend follows the same plan on their phone.

A rest timer that rings on Silent

Log sets and reps in seconds, and hear the rest timer even with your phone on Silent (iOS 26 and later).

Walks, runs and rides count too

Outdoor sessions are tracked, with routes saved to Apple Health.

Not push-ups to unlock. Your actual workout.

Most apps that lock your phone ask for 20 push-ups and call it done. EveryRep's lock follows the program you're on, so the thing that opens your apps is the workout you planned.

How EveryRep compares to other ways of blocking apps →

Your training stays on your iPhone

No account, no sign-in, no ads. Your workouts, program and Apple Health data are stored on your iPhone. EveryRep never sees which apps you use or for how long. Online AI workouts are optional: the app asks first, and works without them.
