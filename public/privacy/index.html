Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

EveryRep is built so your training data stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your profile, program, workouts, runs and routes, places, food log, meal photos, vitamins, fasting, and anything read from Apple Health are stored on your iPhone only. The focus lock uses Apple's Screen Time; EveryRep never sees which apps you use or for how long.

What leaves your iPhone, and only when you say yes

The first time EveryRep would write workouts with AI, it asks. If you choose Use AI (you can change this anytime in Settings > Data & privacy > Online AI):

  • AI workouts. To write a workout, EveryRep sends your goals, schedule, equipment, recent training, injury areas to avoid, age, sex, height, weight, and sleep hours from Apple Health to the EveryRep server.
  • Meal photos. When a photo can't be read on your iPhone, the photo is sent to the EveryRep server to identify the foods.

The EveryRep server passes the request to our AI provider, TODO_PROVIDER, returns the answer, and keeps nothing. TODO_PROVIDER TODO_RETENTION and does not use them to train its models. Requests carry no name, email, Apple ID or device identifier.

If you choose Not now, workouts are built on your iPhone and nothing above is sent.

Things you choose to share

  • Train together. When you share a program by QR code or link, the program (exercises, sets, reps, rest, and the workout title) is stored on the EveryRep server so your friend can open it. It contains no personal details and is deleted after 30 days.
  • Training now. While a workout is running, the app tells the server "training" or "not training" so it can show how many people are training right now. Only the count is kept, in memory.

Apple Health

EveryRep reads sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, weight and steps to show them in the app, and saves the weight you enter and your outdoor runs and rides. Apple Health data is never used for advertising, never sold, and only leaves your iPhone as described above, with your permission.

Deleting your data

Settings > Data & privacy > Delete account & data erases everything on your iPhone. The server keeps nothing from AI requests; shared programs expire after 30 days.

Children

EveryRep is not directed at children under 13.
